About

I am a master's student at the University of Chinese Academy of Sciences, advised by Prof. Xiangsheng Huang, and expect to graduate in June 2027. I am interested in how multimodal models learn representations, adapt to new tasks, and use experience to reason and act.

My recent work, FiberTune, studies how to preserve transferable visual structure during vision-language-action (VLA) fine-tuning. I also build robot systems and contribute to open-source software.

Research Interests

  • Multimodal representation learning and reasoning
  • Continual learning and adaptation
  • World modeling and embodied intelligence

Publications

FiberTune: Preserving Action-Fiber Visual Residuals in Vision-Language-Action Fine-Tuning
Haihao Lin, Xiangsheng Huang, Xiao Yang, Weibang Zhou, Yiqi Zhang, Bo Yang, Simin Zeng, Jiawei Yang, Zhengyang Wang, Jiahui Du.
Accepted at CoRL 2026. First and corresponding author.  [arXiv]  [PDF]  [Project]  [Code]
FiberTune preserves transferable visual structure during VLA fine-tuning, without adding inference-time overhead. Evaluation covers CALVIN, LIBERO, and a real-world SO-101 task. It improves CALVIN ABC→D five-step success from 61.4% to 72.1% and SO-101 task success from 72.7% to 78.1% over task-loss-only fine-tuning.
